Service Warranty & Satisfaction Guarantee

We take pride in our workmanship and stand behind the quality of our services. This warranty outlines what is covered and how concerns are handled.

Our Guarantee
  • If you are not satisfied with the service provided, notify us within 24 hours of service completion.
  • We will review the concern and, if applicable, return to address the affected area at no additional cost.
  • This warranty applies only to the specific service performed.
What Is Covered
  • Missed areas directly related to the booked service
  • Incomplete cleaning tasks listed in the service description
  • Application issues caused by technician error
What Is Not Covered
  • Pre-existing damage, wear, stains, scratches, or oxidation
  • Permanent defects that cannot be corrected by detailing
  • Re-soiling caused by driving, weather, or vehicle use after service
  • Issues arising from customer-supplied products
Maintenance & Coatings
  • Maintenance services preserve condition but do not correct prior neglect.
  • Ceramic coatings and sealants require proper aftercare and maintenance.
  • Warranty coverage does not extend to neglect, misuse, or improper washing.

This warranty is limited to service correction only and does not include refunds unless explicitly stated in writing.
